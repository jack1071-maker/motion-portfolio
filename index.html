How it works

The same four steps every time, so you know what you're getting before anything is animated.

1

Brief and assets

Your product, audience, length and where it will run, plus your logo, fonts and screenshots.

2

Storyboard

A numbered storyboard showing each shot and how it turns into the next. Nothing is built until you approve it.

3

Build and review

I animate it and send it for notes. Timing changes are a code edit, not a re-shoot.

4

Sound and delivery

Sound design and music cued to the picture, delivered as a 4K master plus the cuts you need.
