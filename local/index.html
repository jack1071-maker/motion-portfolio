How it works

You see the finished piece before you pay anything.

  1. I make a free sample

    I build a short video or graphic from your own logo, website photos, fonts and colours. Tell me what you want to promote, or I'll pick something from your site.

  2. You see it first

    I send it over as a link you can watch on your phone. Ask for changes to wording, photos or offers. There's no cost and no obligation.

  3. Pay what it's worth

    If you want to use it, pay what you think it's worth to your business. You get the 4K video and full-size graphics, ready to post.

Built in code. Each frame is drawn on an HTML canvas. Rendered at 4K. Frame by frame in headless Chrome, encoded with ffmpeg. Sized for every feed. 9:16 video, 1:1 post, 9:16 story.
